Vision UI "Person Photo 16" — a square portrait photo for avatars, team rows and profile headers.

```jsx
<PersonPhoto16 src="assets/images/avatar-3.png" size={40} radius={12} />
```

- `size` scales the 320px symbol; `radius` rounds it (avatars: 12px, round: '50%').
- `src` is relative to the consuming page; always pass it outside components/photos/.
